Privacy Policy

This page is a starter privacy policy for the ToolBox project and should be reviewed before the website goes public.

Information we collect

The current browser-based tools do not require an account. Files used by the image tools are processed in your browser and are not intentionally uploaded by the tools.

Cookies and advertising

If advertising or analytics are added later, this policy should be updated to explain what third-party services are used and what information they may collect.

Changes

This policy may be updated as ToolBox gains new features and services.
